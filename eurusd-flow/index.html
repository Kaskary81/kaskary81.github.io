Layout

Tabs keep their own screens, chart types and indicators on this device
Screens
Tap a screen to make it active: the toolbar and the strip under each screen change only that screen. SOLO in the strip shows one indicator at a time; ALL shows every indicator at once.

How to use

Window: laid out like DeepCharts. Header: menu, chart type, indicators, properties, $ trading panel; in the middle the symbol, days loaded and timeframe; on the right the session day. Left strip: drawing tools. Bottom bar: Templates, the daily profile presets, overlays, Fabio's three steps, replay and the trading panel switch.

Bottom bar presets: Dly Vol. Profile = a volume profile at every session open (orange value area, khaki outside, magenta POC; POC / VAH / VAL / VWAP dotted to the next open). Dly Delta Profile = the same profile as delta per price (green buying, purple selling). Dly Profile Values = keep every session's POC / VAH / VAL running until price trades back to it (naked levels). Wkly Vol. Profile / Comp. Vol. Profile = week or composite profile on the right of the price scale. VOLUME, VWAP, CVD, FRAMING (one profile per day) switch overlays.

Tools (left strip, shortcuts as in DeepCharts): Pointer (Alt P) selects and moves drawings by their handles, Hand (Alt H) pans, Crosshair (Alt C), Zoom window (Alt Z: drag a box around the part you want to see and it fills the chart; a click zooms in 2×; Shift-click, Alt X or chart menu › Zoom Previous goes back one step; Alt R or a double-click resets), Horizontal Line (Shift H), Vertical Line, Horizontal Ray, Line (Shift L), Ruler (drag: price change in points, ticks and %, bars, time and volume; one at a time), Rectangle (Shift R), Ellipse, Text (Shift T), Pencil (freehand marker), Volume Profile (Shift V), Long / Short position (Shift B / Shift S: one click places it with a 20-tick stop and a 20-tick target; quantity = risk $ ÷ (stop ticks × tick value), set in Properties › Position tool; drag the target, the stop, the entry or the right end), Snap annotation (Ctrl S, snaps to bar open/high/low/close), Erase All Drawing (Ctrl Delete). Right-click (long-press on a phone) a drawing for Copy, Remove, Property, Extended; double-click opens Annotation Settings (rectangles and ellipses: background colour and opacity; line colour with a custom colour, style, width; Save applies, Configurations › Save as default makes it the tool's default; "Open settings after drawing" opens it after each new drawing of that type). Delete removes the selected drawing, Esc goes back to the pointer.

Volume Profile drawing: drag across the bars. Olive box over the range's high and low, a spine 25% in from the left, volume growing right and delta growing left ("Delta and total volume"), dotted POC / VAH / VAL / VWAP lines to the box's right edge. Right-click it for Vbp Type (Volume, Ask bid volume, Delta, Delta and total volume), Align Right, Show Summary (numbers), Extended and width. Delta is tick delta from Dukascopy's ticks: a tick that lifts the price counts as buying, one that drops it as selling.

Properties (gear, Ctrl P): the DeepCharts properties window. Left: Price Settings and each tool (Deep Print, Deep Profile, Deep Profile Values, Deep Stats, Deep Trades, Big Trades, CVD, Volume, VWAP Envelopes, Session Imbalance, Session Marker), with the same groups, names and choices as DeepCharts. Settings belong to the active screen. Apply keeps the window open; OK applies and closes; Reset to defaults resets the page shown. Choices that need data Binance does not give (number of trades, order-by-order data) are greyed out.

Data: Dukascopy 1-minute bid bars with tick volume for every day; the hour still forming comes from Kraken's live trades shifted onto Dukascopy's price, each trade counted as Kraken's average number of Dukascopy ticks and its delta by the same tick rule (a trade above the last one = buying, below = selling); minutes of that hour from before the page opened come from Kraken candles and have no delta (grey). When Dukascopy publishes the hour it replaces the stand-in. Each day is the FX day, 17:00 to 17:00 New York. Data source and session are in Properties (gear). Prices in pips; 10 pipettes = 1 pip.

Fabio steps: 1 Bias: profile framing, one profile per day; value moving up = longs only, down = shorts only; merge days on the same level (MERGE, drag across days; AUTO-MERGE), colour days. 2 Value: 15-minute bars over 10 days with daily profiles, naked levels, VWAP and the composite. 3 Execute: value chart and 1-minute chart.

Trading panel ($): paper trading only. BUY / SELL MKT at the last price with the OCO strategy (stop beyond the signal bar, target 1:1 or 1:2.5; or Effort boxes (Fabio): no target, the stop trails the Deep-M Effort boxes, see below), Flat closes, Cancel removes the stop and target. Lines on the chart show the position, SL and TP with pips. Nothing is sent to any broker.

Fabio · IQCapital video (Nasdaq model): the tools Fabio Valentini uses in the IQCapital video (NQ on DeepCharts), with the video time. Templates › "Fabio · IQCapital video" opens his three charts on NQ.

  • Nasdaq chart (2:11–9:29): Range 40 bars (header timeframe "40 r", 10 points, set at 7:30), Price "Color based on delta": bright green = buyers in control, white = balance, purple = sellers. He reads them for "mean reverting" and "reload" setups and puts his risk to zero when the other side takes control (6:47).
  • Deep Delta pane (5:54): delta of each bar, the part beyond a threshold saturated; a big delta with no price result = absorption (7:05).
  • Deep SP VWap - VP+L (5:03): white VWAP restarting at each swing, and a profile of each finished swing (volume left, delta right): "the volume weighted average is engaging the price". DSP - Sh Term VP: the profile of the latest completed swing, on the newest swing bar (hiding it removes only that right-most profile).
  • Session Imbalance: IB_H / IB_L (white dotted) and IB_M (blue dashed) of the first hour from 09:30 New York.
  • Deep V-Tracker and the ▲▼ absorption markers (on screen 4:33–5:50): levels where heavy aggression got no result (crimson = buyers absorbed, teal = sellers absorbed) and cyan cluster boxes.
  • Session VP+L (left of the price scale) and DP: Vol,Multiple 1 D (D-VP, right of it, Fabio blue): context; he deletes D-VP at 7:26.
  • Deep-M Effort (added at 7:43 and 22:44 from the Indicator List): green / purple boxes where one side dominates (imbalance, absorption, aggression, path of least resistance) and a smooth line coloured by its slope. His timing and confirmation at the close of the bar that prints the box ("when the sellers take control from the box", 8:15); stacked boxes = extra confluence (8:34); no boxes = no trade (28:39).
  • Deep Trades chart (9:30–13:36): 1-minute hollow candles with Big Trades bubbles (hover or tap a bubble: side, size, price, time). He reads absorbed aggression and trails "below the last aggression" (9:56).
  • OrderTrack chart (13:40–end): Order Flow Analyzer bars (body, delta ladder, volume profile, POC, VAH / VAL). He takes the value-area switch only when the bar has the right colour (16:23), stop beyond the other value-area edge, target 1:1. The value switch markers are optional (Indicator List).
  • Position tool (B$ / S$, 8:23 and 19:21): stop and target 20 ticks, quantity from a fixed $ risk (Properties › Position tool).
  • Effort-box trailing (30:45–31:25): trading panel › "Effort boxes (Fabio)". Box 1 is the first Deep-M Effort box of your side printed by the last bar closed when you enter (the box you enter on) or a later bar; when the 2nd box prints, the stop goes to break-even, the entry price (costs stay in the P/L); at each new box it goes one row beyond the previous box; no partial profits. The stop path is drawn as a dashed step line ("BE", "box 2", …). It uses the boxes of the active screen and never the bar still forming.
  • Indicators panel (header Indicators, Ctrl I; 5:42): the studies of the active screen by chart area; eye = hide / show, gear = properties, trash = remove. The green Indicators button opens the Indicator List (7:43): categories, search, ? (what it shows) and + (add).

Deep-M Effort and Deep V-Tracker (and Deep SP VWap, DSP - Sh Term VP) are our approximations of private DeepCharts studies, built from what the video shows; they are not the original formulas. Everything here is a reading aid for practice, not financial advice; nothing is sent to a broker.
