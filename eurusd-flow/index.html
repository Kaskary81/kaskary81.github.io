How to use

Session (NQ / ES, in the days box: click the open tab of the title bar, or the title bar itself in Group Mode): All hours = the whole electronic day, about 19:00 to 19:00 New York (the stand-in market also trades at weekends). Cash = only the cash session, 09:30 to 16:00 New York, Monday to Friday: every bar, profile, VWAP, CVD, Deep study and big trade on that screen is then worked out on those hours, and weekend days are left out. On Cash the days box counts cash sessions (3D on a Monday = Thursday, Friday and Monday; on a Sunday = Wednesday to Friday); the previous day (P-VP, yesterday's value area, prior-day high / low / close) is the last weekday with a cash session; the weekly and composite profiles, the TPO profile (letter A = the first cash hour) and single prints use the cash sessions only; range bars start afresh at each 09:30 open; a Session Imbalance whose start lies outside 09:30-16:00 (the default 08:00) is measured from 09:30; replay steps over the closed hours; drawings and trades placed in the closed hours are drawn at the edge of their session; the bar countdown is hidden while the cash session is closed. NQ / ES keep the last 20 calendar days, so 20D on Cash shows about 14 sessions. Each screen keeps its own choice. In Fabio's video the chart shows bars before the open and after the close (the whole day), and his Initial Balance is measured from 09:30 New York; the video does not show which hours his profiles use. Practice trades keep running on every minute either way.

Window: laid out like DeepCharts. Control bar at the top: the Flow mark, then the menus. New: Price Chart and Profile Chart open a new tab (candles, or one profile per day). Options: Alerts (Alert Sound, Message Popup and Trading Sound switches), Replay Tick Data (Ctrl Alt E: the window with the session day and the replay buttons, slider, speed and clock), Settings (Properties), Skin/Theme Settings (data, colour theme and sync) and Tool, which holds every command of this app: Session day, Cash session only (NQ / ES), Day plan, BTC funding carry, Forward test, GEX, 4D profile model, Right Profile, Overlays (Volume, EMA, VWAP, CVD, Framing and, on BTC / NQ / ES, Big Trades, GEX and the others), Profile framing (Merge, Unmerge all, Auto-merge, the day colours, Vol), Dim order flow and NQ futures / XM offset (NQ / ES; also in the Feed pill), Alert Settings, Layout, New tab, Full screen, Sync between devices, All indicator toggles, Reset view and Erase All Drawing. Trading: Show Orders (the practice trades). Window: Reset open windows (Ctrl Alt R closes every open window). The chart menu (right-click) ends at Properties, as in DeepCharts. About us: the version and what changed. ?: How to use. Items in grey come in a later step; their tooltip says so. Next to the menus: an amber pill when an update is ready, and an amber Replay pill with the replay time while a past moment is shown (click: the Replay window). On the right: the magnifier (Ctrl K, search; on a phone: Menu › Search), Workspace (comes in a later step) and the Feed pill: green with the feed's name = connected; amber "proxy" = NQ / ES from the trade.xyz stand-in (always marked); amber with a blinking dot = connecting; grey = offline or closed; ⚠ = the server record is incomplete. Hover it for the notes, click it for the notes, the futures offset, Dim order flow and Feed Settings. On a phone the menus sit under Menu. Chart title bar: ☰ shows or hides the drawing strip, chart type, Indicators, Properties, $ Trading Enabled (green square = on), camera (later step); in the middle the chart tabs in DeepCharts' Compact View: one segment per tab named by its symbol and interval ("NQ 5 m"), the open one green, the round + adds a tab, and tabs that do not fit sit behind "+N". Click the open tab (or right-click any tab) for its pop-up: the contract (Ctrl C), the days loaded with, on NQ / ES, the session (Cash or All), the interval (Space opens the interval panel: Minute and Range take any number, press Enter; 1 D = daily profiles; the other bar types come later), Detach (later step) and Close; two tabs with the same symbol and interval read "NQ 1 m" and "NQ 1 m · 2", hover shows the name, and Options › Tool › Layout (screens)… sets 1, 2, 3, 4 or 6 screens. Properties › Data, colour theme and sync › Chart tabs › Group Mode shows the three drop-downs in the title bar instead, with a chevron box that lists the charts of the window (the open one green, × closes, Add chart). Left strip: drawing tools. Bottom bar: the Indicators Toolbar switch (monitor), Templates (with Fabio's steps; a menu until the template file picker comes), the eight presets, DOM Trading (later step) and the Trading panel switch. The app reopens on the step or tab you left.

Feed and data quality: the Feed pill in the control bar names the feed, and the bottom-left of each chart reads "Flow® - feed". NQ / ES come from trade.xyz on Hyperliquid, a stand-in for the CME futures with about one thousandth of their volume: the pill always reads "proxy" in amber and its notes say so, and the footprint and OrderTrack bars, the Deep Delta and CVD panes, big trades, absorption markers, stacked imbalances, Effort zones and the Deep studies are drawn dimmed (the Feed pill or Options › Tool › Dim order flow on proxy data switches it off; delta-coloured candles and delta profiles keep their colours). Time and price studies (profiles, POC / VAH / VAL, TPO, IB, ranges) are fine. A thin strip under the bars marks data quality: grey = no buy / sell side, amber = rows estimated from the 1-minute candle. Range bars are built from 1-minute bars, so they are approximate. Futures offset (NQ / ES): trade.xyz follows the cash index, so NQ and ES futures trade above it by interest minus dividends until expiry. The Feed pill's notes show the estimated offset to the front contract; the Feed pill or Options › Tool › NQ futures / XM offset… switches it off, takes your own offset from your broker's quote, and can show every price label as futures (the data, drawings and alerts stay on the chart's own prices).

Two symbols: Layout (⚙ next to the tabs) › Symbol of each screen. Screen 1 follows the contract box of the title bar; another screen can show NQ, ES, BTC or EUR/USD with its own data, drawings and settings, for example NQ and ES side by side. Practice trading stays on the title-bar symbol. The other screen's title bar has its own session day, a compact Feed pill (notes, futures / XM offset, Dim order flow) and Reset view.

Alerts: Options › Alerts switches the alert sound, message popup and trading sound; Options › Tool › Alert Settings… holds the switches every alert follows (sound, message, notification, reading alerts aloud, trading sound on practice fills), the volume, Discard nearby sounds, and the sound list with your own sound files (kept on this device). Studies and price alerts choose their sound from that list.

Market Profile (TPO): Indicators › Market Profile (TPO) (settings with the gear, as every study; Ctrl K finds the study and each of its settings), or the TPO Profile preset (TPO) in the bottom bar. Every price between a 30-minute period's high and low gets that period's letter (A = 09:30 New York in the RTH profile, 18:00 in the ETH profile, then B, C … Z, a, b …); letters show when you zoom in, blocks otherwise (Display type: Profile draws one bar per row). Sessions on the New York clock: ETH = 18:00 the evening before to 09:30, RTH = 09:30-16:00 Monday to Friday except US market holidays (13:00 on early-close days), post-close to 18:00 only in Triple; a weekend or holiday date has one ETH profile and no RTH. Filter / Split Time: Splitted (ETH + RTH, the default on All hours), Filter (RTH only; Cash screens are always RTH only), Triple, None (one profile per trading day, 18:00 to 18:00), or your own times in 15-minute steps (the window may cross midnight). The study works on a fixed row (Tick Grouping, per symbol), so POC, value area and every level stay put when you zoom; the screen only merges rows that get too small. POC row, value area (70 % or more of the TPOs, Classic or Single-row method), initial balance = the first 60 minutes, single prints, a session candle beside each profile. Deep Windo annotations (PRO) add the session header, an estimated open type after 10:30 (OD, OTD, ORR, OA), range bars, a mirrored volume profile with the VPOC in yellow, labelled levels (VAH, VAL, POC, VPOC, IB and its 1.5x / 2x extensions, halfback, overnight high / low over the ETH part, or from the prior RTH close by setting), the auction marks (single prints, tails with signed block counts, poor high / low at the real high / low, ledges when chosen, and one block with the prior RTH close, the gap and the overnight inventory: LONG, SHORT, or NEUTRAL from 40 % to 60 %), a current-price line on the live profile and the levels of closed profiles running on until touched (T). Labels are drawn over the candles, inside the chart, below the bar values line, and never on another profile or session candle; every level line is drawn, and only a label with no room is left out. The right-hand TPO profile uses the same rows and starts at 09:30, and the Deep Profile can be split ETH / RTH too (its settings › Filter / Split Time; merged days stay merged per part).

Lower panes: Deep Delta, Volume and CVD each have Chart area in their settings: 2 is the first pane under the price, 3 the next, and the same number puts two studies in one pane, the second with its own scale (Use sec. axis).

NQ / ES screens start on the Cash session; before the open (and at weekends) the last cash session is shown, and today's own studies (session profile, IB, value area) start at 09:30 New York.

D-VL Dly Profile Values = keep every session's POC / VAH / VAL running until price trades back to it (naked levels). C-VP = the composite profile right of the price scale. Tools (left strip, shortcuts as in DeepCharts): in DeepCharts' order. Pointer (Alt P) selects and moves drawings by their handles, Hand (Alt H) pans, Crosshair (Alt C), Global Crosshair (Alt G: the crosshair on every screen of this window, same time and price; screens of another symbol run in their own frame and are left out), Zoom Range (Alt R, or Alt Z: drag a box around the part you want to see and it fills the chart; a click zooms in 2×; Shift-click, Alt X or chart menu › Zoom Out goes back one step; a double-click or Options › Tool › Reset view resets), the eye (hides or shows the drawings, in every screen of the window including second-symbol screens; they are kept, and drawing anything shows them again), Erase All Drawing (Ctrl Delete), Snap annotation (the magnet, Ctrl S), Horizontal Line (Shift H), Vertical Line, Horizontal Ray, Line (Shift L), Parallel Lines (Shift P: drag the first line, then drag the middle dot of the second line to set the channel width), Ruler (drag: price change in points, ticks and %, bars, time and volume; one at a time), Rectangle (Shift R), Ellipse, Text (Shift T), Pen (freehand marker), Price Retracement (drag from the start to the end of the move: 0, 23.6, 38.2, 50, 61.8, 78.6 and 100 %), Price Projection (100, 161.8 and 261.8 % of the move), Price Fan (Shift F: 38.2, 50 and 61.8 % fan lines), Volume Profile (Shift V), a tool not yet named (grey), Buy / Sell calculator (Shift B / Shift S: one click places it with a 20-tick stop and a 20-tick target; quantity = risk $ ÷ (stop ticks × tick value), set in Properties › Position tool; drag the target, the stop, the entry or the right end), Snap annotation (Ctrl S, snaps to bar open/high/low/close), Erase All Drawing (Ctrl Delete). Right-click (long-press on a phone) a drawing for Copy, Remove, Property, Extended; double-click opens Annotation Settings (rectangles and ellipses: background colour and opacity; line colour with a custom colour, style, width; Save applies, Configurations › Save as default makes it the tool's default; "Open settings after drawing" opens it after each new drawing of that type). Delete removes the selected drawing, Esc goes back to the pointer.

Volume Profile drawing: drag across the bars (Shift V). Drawn as Fabio draws it in DeepCharts: thin olive box over the range's high and low, the spine on its left edge, volume growing right (orange value area, pale yellow outside) and delta growing left outside the box (green buying, purple selling), red POC row with a magenta line to the right edge, yellow VWAP row with a dotted line, dashed grey VAH / VAL, white handles. Right-click it for Vbp Type (Volume, Ask bid volume, Delta, Delta and total volume), Vbp Data (Auto; Traded prices only; Traded prices + minute range: on the thin NQ / ES feed half of each minute's volume stays at its traded prices and half is spread over the minute's high-low, so the profile is continuous), Row Size (Auto picks the finest row that fills the range), Align Right, Show Text, Show Summary, Extended and width. POC and value area keep 5-point rows on NQ / ES, so one big single trade does not become the POC.

Properties (gear, Ctrl P): the DeepCharts properties window. Left: Price Settings and each tool (Deep Print, Deep Profile, Deep Profile Values, Deep Stats, Deep Trades, Big Trades, CVD, Volume, VWAP Envelopes, Session Imbalance, Session Marker), with the same groups, names and choices as DeepCharts. Settings belong to the active screen. Apply keeps the window open; OK applies and closes; Reset to defaults resets the page shown. Choices that need data Binance does not give (number of trades, order-by-order data) are greyed out.

Data: Dukascopy 1-minute bid bars with tick volume for every day; the hour still forming comes from Kraken's live trades shifted onto Dukascopy's price, each trade counted as Kraken's average number of Dukascopy ticks and its delta by the same tick rule (a trade above the last one = buying, below = selling); minutes of that hour from before the page opened come from Kraken candles and have no delta (grey). When Dukascopy publishes the hour it replaces the stand-in. Each day is the FX day, 17:00 to 17:00 New York. Data source and session are in Properties (gear). Prices in pips; 10 pipettes = 1 pip.

Fabio steps (Templates › Fabio steps; the app opens on your chart tab, never on a step): 1 Bias: profile framing, one profile per day; value moving up = longs only, down = shorts only; merge days on the same level (MERGE, drag across days; AUTO-MERGE), colour days. 2 Value: 15-minute bars over 10 days with daily profiles, naked levels, VWAP and the composite. 3 Execute: value chart and 1-minute chart.

Trading panel (bottom-bar switch, Ctrl Alt P; $ in the title bar = Trading Enabled, Ctrl Alt T): practice trading only, marked PRACTICE; Broker and Account read Practice and nothing is sent to any broker. From the top: the instrument (the contract list), Open Qty, Open P/L, Daily P/L, Qty with its arrows (Risk comes later), Broker, Account, Ticks diff. (the distance of LMT and STP orders from the last price, in ticks). BUY / SELL: MKT at the last price with the Mode below; BID / ASK = a limit order one tick better than the last price; LMT = a limit order Ticks diff. away; STP = a stop order Ticks diff. away; STP LMT comes later. Pending orders show on the chart (drag to move, × to cancel) and fill on the live price. Cancel removes every pending order, stop and target; Breakeven moves the stop to the entry only on a trade at least one tick in profit (otherwise nothing changes and a short message says why); Cancel and Flat also closes every position. OCO Strategy on = each trade gets the Mode's stop and target (SL + TP 1:1 or 1:2.5, or Effort boxes (Fabio): no target, the stop trails the Deep-M Effort boxes, see below); off = no stop or target. SL. / TP. / BE. / Trail. in ticks and Link pending orders come later. Lines on the chart show the position, SL and TP with pips.

Fabio · IQCapital video (Nasdaq model): the tools Fabio Valentini uses in the IQCapital video (NQ on DeepCharts), with the video time. Templates › "Fabio · IQCapital video" opens his three charts on NQ.

  • Nasdaq chart (2:11–9:29): Range 40 bars (interval box "40 Range", 10 points, set at 7:30), Price "Color based on delta": bright green = buyers in control, white = balance, purple = sellers. He reads them for "mean reverting" and "reload" setups and puts his risk to zero when the other side takes control (6:47).
  • Deep Delta pane (5:54): delta of each bar, the part beyond a threshold saturated; a big delta with no price result = absorption (7:05).
  • Deep SP VWap - VP+L (5:03): white VWAP restarting at each swing, and a profile of each finished swing (volume left, delta right): "the volume weighted average is engaging the price". DSP - Sh Term VP: the profile of the latest completed swing, on the newest swing bar (hiding it removes only that right-most profile).
  • Session Imbalance: IB_H / IB_L (white dotted) and IB_M (blue dashed) of the first hour from 09:30 New York.
  • Deep V-Tracker and the ▲▼ absorption markers (on screen 4:33–5:50): levels where heavy aggression got no result (crimson = buyers absorbed, teal = sellers absorbed) and cyan cluster boxes.
  • Session VP+L (left of the price scale) and DP: Vol,Multiple 1 D (D-VP, right of it, Fabio blue): context; he deletes D-VP at 7:26.
  • Deep-M Effort (added at 7:43 and 22:44 from the Indicator List): green / purple boxes where one side dominates (imbalance, absorption, aggression, path of least resistance) and a smooth line coloured by its slope. His timing and confirmation at the close of the bar that prints the box ("when the sellers take control from the box", 8:15); stacked boxes = extra confluence (8:34); no boxes = no trade (28:39).
  • Deep Trades chart (9:30–13:36): 1-minute hollow candles with Big Trades bubbles (hover or tap a bubble: side, size, price, time). He reads absorbed aggression and trails "below the last aggression" (9:56).
  • OrderTrack chart (13:40–end): Order Flow Analyzer bars (body, delta ladder, volume profile, POC, VAH / VAL). He takes the value-area switch only when the bar has the right colour (16:23), stop beyond the other value-area edge, target 1:1. The value switch markers are optional (Indicator List).
  • Position tool (B$ / S$, 8:23 and 19:21): stop and target 20 ticks, quantity from a fixed $ risk (Properties › Position tool).
  • Effort-box trailing (30:45–31:25): trading panel › "Effort boxes (Fabio)". Box 1 is the first Deep-M Effort box of your side printed by the last bar closed when you enter (the box you enter on) or a later bar; when the 2nd box prints, the stop goes to break-even, the entry price (costs stay in the P/L); at each new box it goes one row beyond the previous box; no partial profits. The stop path is drawn as a dashed step line ("BE", "box 2", …). It uses the boxes of the active screen and never the bar still forming.
  • Indicators panel (bar-graph icon of the title bar, Ctrl I; 5:42): the studies of the active screen by chart area; eye = hide / show, gear = properties, trash = remove; Esc or the icon closes it. (what it shows) and + (add); on the right the studies of the screen: drag ≡ to change their order, − removes, the gear opens the settings.

Deep-M Effort and Deep V-Tracker (and Deep SP VWap, DSP - Sh Term VP) are our approximations of private DeepCharts studies, built from what the video shows; they are not the original formulas. Everything here is a reading aid for practice, not financial advice; nothing is sent to a broker.
